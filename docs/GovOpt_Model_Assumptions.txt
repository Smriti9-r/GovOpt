
GOVOPT - MODEL METHODOLOGY AND ASSUMPTIONS
==========================================

1. PROJECT SCOPE
District-level government resource allocation decision-support
for the 13 districts of Uttarakhand.

2. DATA
- Education: reference period 2024-25.
- Water: assessment year 2024.
- Healthcare: reporting year not confirmed.
- Population: 2024 projected population estimates.
- Population source: Esri India Living Atlas, as recorded
  in the supplied population dataset.

3. NEED SCORE
- Education weight: 50%.
- Water weight: 50%.
- Education proxy: min-max scaled average enrolment per school.
- Water dimensions:
  a. Adequate quantity gap.
  b. Access/reliability gap: average of functional connections
     gap and regular supply gap.
  c. Satisfaction with quantity gap.
- Each water dimension receives equal weight.
- Higher score indicates higher relative measured need.

4. ALLOCATION SCENARIOS
Illustrative total budget: Rs. 500 crore.
Minimum guarantee: Rs. 20 crore per district.
Maximum allocation in baseline optimisation: Rs. 70 crore.
These are demonstration assumptions, not official limits.

5. BASELINE OPTIMISATION
Objective: maximise the sum of district need score multiplied
by district allocation.
Constraints:
- Total allocation = Rs. 500 crore.
- Minimum allocation = Rs. 20 crore per district.
- Maximum allocation = Rs. 70 crore per district.

6. POPULATION-ADJUSTED SCENARIOS
The remaining budget after minimum guarantees is distributed
using blended population and need-score shares:
- 30% population + 70% need.
- 50% population + 50% need.
- 70% population + 30% need.

7. LIMITATIONS
- Need score is an analyst-defined preliminary index.
- Average enrolment per school is not a direct measure of
  overcrowding or school capacity.
- Healthcare indicators are not fully incorporated into
  the need score.
- Healthcare capacity and intervention-cost data remain
  incomplete.
- Population figures are projections, not a new census.
- Allocation weights and budget limits are illustrative.
- Results are decision-support scenarios, not official
  government funding recommendations.
- Indicator definitions, denominators and reporting periods
  require further verification.

8. VALIDATION
All allocation scenarios must cover 13 districts and
sum to the Rs. 500 crore budget.
